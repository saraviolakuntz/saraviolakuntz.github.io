About me

I am currently a research associate at the Chair of Information-oriented Control (ITR) at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), working with Sandra Hirche within the DFG Research Training Group METEOR on machine learning and control theory.

I did my doctoral research with Christian Kuehn, also at TUM, on the dynamics and geometry of neural networks.
